        But how does Claude make these animated videos?


           1. No AI video tool is involved: Claude writes code that draws

             every frame.

           2. It studies your references and copies the style, not the subject.

           3. It plans a mini-film: a hook, a build-up, a payoff.

           4. Chrome, already on your computer, turns the code into

             frames.

           5. Claude checks sample frames and fixes what looks off.

           6. It composes the soundtrack in code, synced to the picture.

           7. A free tool (ffmpeg) joins it all into a ready-to-post MP4.
